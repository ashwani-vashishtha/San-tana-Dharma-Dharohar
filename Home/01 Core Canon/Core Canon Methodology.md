> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the editorial methodology used to build, evaluate, review, and maintain the **Core Canon of 108 Essential Sādhana Resources**.

---

# Objective

The Core Canon is intended to identify the minimum authentic corpus of scriptures, liturgical texts, philosophical works, and sādhana resources that provide a comprehensive foundation for the serious study and practice of Sanātana Dharma.

The objective is **not** to create a list of the most popular Hindu texts.

The objective is to create the most authentic, balanced, evidence-based, and defensible canonical collection possible.

---

# Scope

The Core Canon includes resources that contribute significantly to:

- Understanding Sanātana Dharma
- Spiritual study (Adhyayana)
- Spiritual practice (Sādhana)
- Preservation of traditional knowledge
- Living religious practice

The Core Canon is intentionally selective.

It is not intended to include every important work within the tradition.

---

# Editorial Principles

## 1. Pramāṇa First

Scriptural authority takes precedence over modern popularity.

---

## 2. Practice First

Resources should contribute to genuine study or authentic sādhana.

---

## 3. Non-sectarian, Tradition-aware

The Canon should represent the diversity of authentic Sanātana Dharma while clearly identifying traditional contexts.

---

## 4. Historical Influence

Preference is given to resources that have shaped Hindu thought and practice across generations.

---

## 5. Living Relevance

Resources that remain actively studied, recited, or practiced receive greater consideration.

---

## 6. Authenticity over Completeness

The objective is quality rather than quantity.

When uncertainty exists, the resource should remain outside the Core Canon until sufficient justification is established.

---

# Definition of a Sādhana Resource

A Sādhana Resource is any authoritative textual or liturgical work that substantially contributes to the understanding, practice, preservation, or realization of Sanātana Dharma.

Examples include:

- Scriptures
- Upaniṣads
- Stotras
- Sahasranāmas
- Vedic Sūktas
- Mantras
- Kavachas
- Darśana texts
- Yoga scriptures
- Ritual manuals
- Āgamas
- Tantras

---

# Inclusion Criteria

Each candidate should be evaluated using the following criteria.

| Criterion | Importance |
|------------|-----------|
| Scriptural Authority | Essential |
| Traditional Acceptance | Essential |
| Practical Sādhana Value | Essential |
| Historical Influence | High |
| Availability of Authentic Source | High |
| Cross-Sampradāya Significance | Medium |

---

# Editorial Workflow

Every candidate follows the same process.

## Phase 1

Discovery

Collect potential candidates from authentic sources.

---

## Phase 2

Evaluation

Assess each candidate against the inclusion criteria.

---

## Phase 3

Justification

Document why the resource deserves inclusion.

Every inclusion must be defensible.

---

## Phase 4

Review

Review the complete Canon for:

- balance
- representation
- authenticity
- overlap
- omissions

---

## Phase 5

Approval

Only after review does a resource become part of the Core Canon.

---

# Canon vs Curriculum

The Core Canon answers:

> What belongs in the repository?

The Curriculum answers:

> In what order should a seeker study these resources?

These are independent decisions.

Authority determines the Canon.

Pedagogy determines the Curriculum.

---

# Core Canon vs Extended Canon

## Core Canon

The minimum authentic corpus that every serious student of Sanātana Dharma should know or engage with.

---

## Extended Canon

Additional important resources that are:

- specialized
- regional
- lineage-specific
- advanced
- supplementary

A resource excluded from the Core Canon is **not** necessarily unimportant.

---

# Editorial Rule

When in doubt,

> Prefer inclusion in the Extended Canon rather than weakening the Core Canon.

---

# Repository Goal

Every inclusion should answer two questions:

1. Why does this resource belong?

2. Why is it preferred over similar candidates?

If these questions cannot be answered satisfactorily, the resource should not enter the Core Canon.
