# Current Editorial Architecture

The repository follows a three-layer knowledge architecture.

## Layer 1

Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला)

The curated collection of foundational textual works.

---

## Layer 2

Jīvanta Paramparā (जीवन्त परम्परा)

The living practice collection containing curated liturgical, ritual, devotional, and practice-oriented resources.

---

## Layer 3

Dharma Sāgara (धर्म सागर)

The comprehensive preservation layer containing advanced, regional, historical, lineage-specific, and research material.
