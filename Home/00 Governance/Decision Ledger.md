# Purpose

This ledger records all governance, editorial, structural, and architectural decisions made during the development of **Sanātana Dharma Dharohar**.

It serves as the authoritative historical record of accepted project decisions.

Superseded decisions are intentionally retained to preserve the evolution of the repository and provide historical context.

---

# Usage

The Decision Ledger should be updated whenever a governance decision is made that affects:

- Repository architecture
- Editorial methodology
- Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला)
- Inclusion principles
- Taxonomy
- Editorial standards
- Repository governance

Routine editorial work should **not** be recorded here.

## DL-037 — Ritual Procedures vs Textual Works

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-29

### Decision

The Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला) represents individual textual works only.

Ritual procedures, liturgical practices, and practical observances—including Pūjā, Homa, Abhiṣeka, Prayoga, and Saṁskāras—are not independent Ādhāra Śilā works.

Such material belongs within **Jīvanta Paramparā** as curated practice collections.

### Reason

Maintaining a clear distinction between textual sources and living practices preserves the integrity of the repository's three-layer knowledge architecture.

---

## DL-036 — Foundational Kavaca Selection Principle

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-29

### Decision

Only foundational and broadly authoritative Kavaca texts shall be eligible for inclusion within the Ādhāra Śilā.

Specialized, regional, lineage-specific, or sectarian Kavacas shall be preserved within Dharma Sāgara.

### Reason

This preserves the representative nature of the Ādhāra Śilā while allowing comprehensive preservation elsewhere in the repository.

---

## DL-035 — Project Handover as AI Handshake Protocol

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-29

### Decision

The file `Project Handover.md` is designated as the official synchronization document between the Obsidian repository and AI assistants.

Every new AI session shall begin by reading the handover before consulting other governance documents.

The handover shall summarize:

- Current repository state
- Governance snapshot
- Frozen architecture
- Current milestone
- Current work package
- Session delta
- Synchronization checklist
- Recommended starting point

### Reason

Separating long-term governance from session continuity improves consistency between AI-assisted editing sessions while reducing redundant onboarding.

## DL-034 — Scope of Jīvanta Paramparā

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-27

### Decision

Jīvanta Paramparā may contain curated practice collections including, but not limited to:

- Nitya Pūjā
- Sandhyāvandana
- Havan
- Prayoga
- Saṁskāras
- Śānti Mantras
- Temple Liturgies
- Daily Recitation Collections

These collections are intentionally different from the textual works that comprise Ādhāra Śilā.

---

## DL-033 — Master Candidate Inclusion Principle

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-27

### Decision

Every Master Candidate entry must represent a distinct identifiable textual work.

The following shall not be included as independent candidates:

- Literary genres
- Ritual categories
- Thematic collections

These belong within Jīvanta Paramparā.

---

## DL-032 — Three-Layer Knowledge Architecture

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-27

### Decision

The repository shall use a three-layer editorial architecture.

1. Ādhāra Śilā
2. Jīvanta Paramparā
3. Dharma Sāgara

### Reason

Separate foundational textual sources from living practice while allowing unlimited future expansion.

---

## DL-031 — Classification by Primary Contribution

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

Works within the Ādhāra Śilā shall be classified according to their primary intellectual, philosophical, devotional, or practical contribution to Sanātana Dharma rather than by their titles, historical placement, or secondary themes.

### Examples

- Yoga Vāsiṣṭha → Mokṣa Śāstra
- Bhagavad Gītā → Mokṣa Śāstra
- Viṣṇu Sahasranāma → Sahasranāma Literature
- Devī Māhātmya → Purāṇa Literature

### Reason

This principle establishes a stable editorial rule for classifying works according to their dominant contribution to the tradition rather than incidental subject matter. It ensures consistency across the repository, avoids duplication, and preserves a coherent and enduring editorial taxonomy.
