# Purpose

This ledger records all governance, editorial, structural, and architectural decisions made during the development of **Sanātana Dharma Dharohar**.

It serves as the authoritative historical record of accepted project decisions.

Superseded decisions are intentionally retained to preserve the evolution of the repository and provide historical context.

---

# Usage

The Decision Ledger should be updated whenever a governance decision is made that affects:

- Repository architecture
- Editorial methodology
- Ādhāra Śilā (Foundational Sources)
- Inclusion principles
- Taxonomy
- Editorial standards
- Repository governance

Routine editorial work should **not** be recorded here.

# Decisions

## DL-025 — Section IV Structure Frozen

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-23

### Decision

The structure of **Section IV – Dharma, Darśana & Philosophical Traditions (धर्म, दर्शन एवं दार्शनिक परम्पराएँ)** is frozen.

Section IV shall comprise:

- **IV-A. Dharma Literature (धर्म साहित्य)**
  - Dharma Sūtras
  - Dharma Śāstras
  - Nīti Literature

- **IV-B. Ṣaḍ-Darśanas (षड्दर्शन)**
  - Nyāya
  - Vaiśeṣika
  - Sāṅkhya
  - Yoga Darśana
  - Pūrva Mīmāṃsā
  - Vedānta (represented by the *Brahma Sūtra*)

- **IV-C. Vedānta (वेदान्त)**
  - Advaita (अद्वैत)
  - Viśiṣṭādvaita (विशिष्टाद्वैत)
  - Dvaita (द्वैत)

The **Brahma Sūtra** represents Vedānta as one of the Ṣaḍ-Darśanas in Section IV-B. The principal Vedānta traditions and their representative literature are curated separately in Section IV-C.

Section headings and philosophical traditions shall use the editorial convention:

**English / IAST (Devanāgarī)**

Future revisions to Section IV should normally be limited to:

- candidate works,
- editorial status,
- Ādhāra Śilā allocation,

and should not alter the overall structure without a new governance decision.

### Reason

This decision finalizes the editorial architecture of Section IV, ensuring a clear distinction between the classical philosophical system of Vedānta and its principal interpretative traditions. It establishes a stable framework for future editorial work while maintaining consistency with the repository's representation model and governance principles.

## DL-026 — Ādhāra Śilā Terminology

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

The repository's foundational knowledge layer shall henceforth be known as **Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला)**.

"Foundational Sources" shall serve as the explanatory English description of this layer.

The previous term **Core Canon** is superseded throughout the repository.

### Reason

The term "Core Canon" implied a fixed and closed canon, which does not accurately reflect the pluralistic and evolving textual traditions of Sanātana Dharma. **Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला)** better expresses the repository's role as the enduring foundation upon which the remaining knowledge architecture is built.

---

## DL-027 — Project Vision & Mission Revision

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

The project's Vision and Mission are revised to emphasize that the repository exists not only to preserve authentic knowledge but also to enable seekers to progress from curiosity to informed and responsible spiritual practice.

### Reason

The project's long-term objective has matured beyond building a structured repository. It seeks to preserve authentic knowledge while making it meaningful and usable for sincere seekers without compromising scholarly rigor or traditional authenticity.

---

## DL-028 — Project Evolution Principle

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

Ideas discovered during research that fall outside the current milestone shall be recorded in the project roadmap or backlog and deferred until the appropriate phase.

Active milestones shall remain focused and shall not be interrupted except when governance decisions require immediate resolution.

### Reason

The repository is intended as a long-term project. Capturing future ideas without interrupting ongoing milestones ensures both disciplined execution and continuous architectural growth.

---

## DL-029 — Sanskrit Editorial Standard

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

Whenever Sanskrit terminology is introduced using IAST transliteration, the corresponding Devanāgarī script shall also be provided wherever appropriate throughout the repository.

### Reason

Providing both IAST and Devanāgarī improves accuracy, accessibility, consistency, and long-term editorial quality while serving both academic readers and traditional practitioners.

## DL-030 — Section V Structure Frozen

**Status:** Frozen

**Date:** 2026-07-26

### Decision

The structure of **Section V – Tantra & Āgama (तन्त्र एवं आगम)** is frozen.

Section V shall comprise:

- **V-A. Śaiva Āgamas (शैव आगम)**
- **V-B. Vaiṣṇava Āgamas (वैष्णव आगम)**
- **V-C. Śākta Tantras (शाक्त तन्त्र)**
- **V-D. General Tantric Literature (सामान्य तान्त्रिक साहित्य)**

These subdivisions establish the permanent editorial architecture for representing the principal Āgamic and Tantric traditions within the repository.

Future revisions to Section V should normally be limited to:

- candidate works,
- editorial status,
- Ādhāra Śilā allocation,

and should not alter the overall structure without a new governance decision.

### Reason

This decision completes the architectural framework for the Tantra & Āgama section before detailed editorial curation begins. It ensures balanced and independent representation of the major Āgamic traditions together with representative Tantric literature while maintaining consistency with the repository's editorial methodology and governance principles.
