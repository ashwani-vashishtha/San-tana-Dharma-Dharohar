# Scope

This entry represents **Śrī Vaiṣṇava Sampradāya — श्रीवैष्णव सम्प्रदाय** as a living Vaiṣṇava Sampradāya associated with:

- Viṣṇu/Nārāyaṇa — विष्णु / नारायण
- Śrī/Lakṣmī — श्री / लक्ष्मी
- Āḻvār — आळ्वार devotional heritage
- Nālāyira Divya Prabandham — नालायिर दिव्यप्रबन्धम्
- Ācārya-paramparā — आचार्य–परम्परा
- Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta — विशिष्टाद्वैत वेदान्त
- temple traditions
- continuing community and institutional expression

This entry documents living tradition, transmission, relationship, and practice context. It does not duplicate canonical or textual content belonging to **Ādhāra Śilā**.

# Boundary Notes

## Rāmānujācārya

Śrī Rāmānujācārya — श्री रामानुजाचार्य is represented as a principal/formative Ācārya and principal exponent of Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta — विशिष्टाद्वैत वेदान्त.

This entry does not simplify the tradition to:

> Śrī Vaiṣṇava Sampradāya → founded by → Rāmānuja

because the approved representation recognizes significant pre-Rāmānuja roots.

## Nāthamuni and Yāmunācārya

Śrī Nāthamuni — श्री नाथमुनि and Śrī Yāmunācārya — श्री यामुनाचार्य are represented with evidence-qualified relationship language.

Traditional Ācārya-paramparā attribution, historical evidence, and scholarly interpretation remain distinct.

## Vaṭakalai and Teṅkalai

Vaṭakalai — वडकलै and Teṅkalai — तेङ्कलै are represented as major internal Śrī Vaiṣṇava schools/streams.

They are not automatically classified as separate Sampradāyas in this entry.

## Gauḍīya Boundary

Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava tradition — गौड़ीय वैष्णव परम्परा remains distinct.

This entry does not create:

- Rāmānuja → Śrī Caitanya guru-paramparā
- Rāmānuja → Caitanya direct succession
- Śrī Vaiṣṇava → Gauḍīya parent-child lineage

The four-sampradāya classification is not converted into an unquestioned timeless repository taxonomy.
