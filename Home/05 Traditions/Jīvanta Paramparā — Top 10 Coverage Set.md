## Selection Principle

The ten traditions were selected to provide broad architectural and civilizational coverage across:

- classical Sampradaya;
- Vaishnava traditions;
- Shaiva traditions;
- Shakta traditions;
- guru-parampara and Yoga traditions;
- monastic/Advaita traditions; and
- temple/civilizational living traditions.

"Top 10" does not mean "ten most important traditions."

The set is not a ranking of spiritual importance, authenticity, antiquity, popularity, canonical status, or superiority of one tradition over another.

## Architectural Coverage

The set deliberately tests different kinds of living continuity:

- Sampradaya
- Acharya-parampara
- Guru-parampara
- Monastic order
- Philosophical lineage
- Temple-centered tradition
- Regional/civilizational tradition
- Contemporary institutional transmission
