# Purpose

The Master Candidate List serves as the comprehensive editorial inventory of textual works considered for inclusion within the **Sanātana Dharma Dharohar** repository.

It is the primary working catalogue from which the **Ādhāra Śilā (Foundational Sources)**, **Extended Sources**, and **Research Archive** are curated.

Inclusion in this list represents editorial consideration only and does **not** imply acceptance into the Ādhāra Śilā.

Editorial evaluation is governed by the Constitution, implemented through the Ādhāra Śilā Methodology, and documented through the Decision Ledger.

---

# Editorial Workflow

Each work normally progresses through the following editorial process:

**Candidate → Editorial Review → Classification → Repository Layer → Repository Placement**

Candidates may move between editorial classifications as research progresses.

Final repository placement is determined only after editorial review.

---

# Editorial Status

The following classifications indicate the editorial importance of a work **during evaluation**. They are **not** the final repository classifications.

| Status | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 🟢 Core Candidate | Essential representative of its tradition and a strong candidate for the Ādhāra Śilā |
| 🔵 Major Candidate | Highly influential work with broad historical or doctrinal significance |
| 🟡 Supplementary Candidate | Valuable for deeper study or specialized traditions |
| ⚪ Reference Candidate | Primarily retained for historical, comparative, or research purposes |

---

# Repository Layer

Following editorial review, accepted works are assigned one of the following repository layers.

| Repository Layer | Purpose |
|------------------|---------|
| Ādhāra Śilā | Essential individual textual works forming the foundational canon of Sanātana Dharma Dharohar |
| Jīvanta Paramparā | Curated living-practice collections representing active practice, liturgy, and ritual use |
| Dharma Sāgara | Complete curated knowledge repository preserving expanded, regional, specialized, and research material |

## IV-D. Mokṣa Śāstra (मोक्षशास्त्र)

Direct teachings whose primary purpose is Self-realization and Liberation (Mokṣa).

These works represent the culmination of spiritual inquiry within Sanātana Dharma. Rather than prescribing social order, philosophical systems, or devotional practice, they guide the seeker toward direct realization of the Self and the highest Truth.

| Work | Status |
|------|--------|
| Bhagavad Gītā | 🟢 |
| Aṣṭāvakra Gītā | 🟢 |
| Yoga Vāsiṣṭha | 🟢 |

> **Editorial Note:** Mokṣa Śāstra represents the culminating wisdom literature of the repository. Works are classified according to their **primary contribution** rather than their titles or historical associations. Thus, texts such as the *Bhagavad Gītā* and *Yoga Vāsiṣṭha* are curated here because Liberation (Mokṣa) constitutes their principal teaching.
