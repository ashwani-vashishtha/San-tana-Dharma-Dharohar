# Purpose

The **Ādhāra Śilā (आधार शिला)** identifies the **108 Foundational Sources of Sanātana Dharma** that every sincere student and seeker should encounter during a lifetime of authentic study and sādhana.

Rather than representing every important work ever composed, the Ādhāra Śilā provides a carefully curated foundation that introduces the principal scriptural, philosophical, devotional, ritual, and contemplative traditions of Sanātana Dharma.

It serves as the foundational knowledge layer of the **Sanātana Dharma Dharohar** repository and is curated in accordance with the Constitution, the Ādhāra Śilā Methodology, and the Ādhāra Śilā Taxonomy.

---

# Editorial Principles

The Ādhāra Śilā is designed to:

- Represent the breadth of Sanātana Dharma fairly and responsibly.
- Remain neutral across Sampradāyas.
- Preserve both scriptural authority and living traditions.
- Balance foundational texts with representative literature.
- Provide a lifelong path **from curiosity to sādhana**.

# Editorial Note

The Ādhāra Śilā is a curated editorial framework rather than an exhaustive bibliography.

Many highly influential works are intentionally preserved within **Jīvanta Paramparā** or **Dharma Sāgara**, ensuring that the Ādhāra Śilā remains focused, balanced, and approachable while still supporting comprehensive study and preservation.
